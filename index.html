About Me

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems and have nearly three years of experience inspiring students at Clarendon College, followed by teaching college students at STI Global City. After earning my teaching license in the Philippines, I expanded my practice to Thailand — first teaching mathematics to young learners at โรงเรียนจินดาพร, and currently teaching Computer Science to high school students at Ramkhamhaeng Advent International School (RAIS).


My unique strength lies at the intersection of technology and art — combining programming, web development, and digital design with creative teaching approaches that make complex concepts approachable and exciting. Whether it's HTML & CSS, Arduino, Python, or Adobe Creative Suite, I believe ICT should empower every learner to create, not just consume.
